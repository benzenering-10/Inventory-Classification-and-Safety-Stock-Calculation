# Inventory Classification and Safety Stock Calculation

## Business Problem

Excess inventory ties up capital while insufficient inventory increases the risk of stockouts. This project develops an inventory classification framework and calculates safety stock levels to support inventory management decisions.

## Objectives

- Classify inventory items based on business importance
- Identify high-priority inventory requiring tighter controls
- Calculate safety stock levels
- Reduce stockout risk while minimizing excess inventory

## Deliverables

- Presentation
- Supporting Excel workbook

## Methodology

### Inventory Classification

Inventory items were classified using ABC analysis based on annual consumption value.

- A Items: Highest business impact
- B Items: Moderate impact
- C Items: Lowest impact

### Safety Stock Calculation

Safety stock was calculated using demand variability and desired service level assumptions.

## Key Findings

- A-items represented a small percentage of SKUs but a majority of inventory value.
- C-items represented most SKUs but minimal inventory value.
- Safety stock recommendations varied significantly across classifications.
